## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

- ❌ **Script Does Not Execute After Pasting**
  This usually means the executor lost injection context. Re-inject your executor while inside the SCP-3008 game lobby, then paste the **3008 Script Pastebin** code again and hit execute.

- ❌ **Auto Build Places Walls in Wrong Positions**
  Outdated templates can misalign after a game update. Redownload the latest **3008 Script Auto Build Pastebin** config files and replace the contents in `C:/Users/YourName/Documents/3008Script/templates/`.

- ❌ **GUI Does Not Appear After Execution**
  Some executors block UI rendering calls. Switch to a higher-level executor or restart Roblox entirely, rejoin the server, and execute the **3008 Script** payload on a fresh session.

- ❌ **Bring Items Teleports Wrong Objects**
  The priority list in config may be misconfigured. Open `config.json` and verify that the `"priority"` array under `bring_items` lists the correct item names in your preferred order.

- ❌ **Lag Spikes When ESP Is Active**
  Lower the `draw_distance` value from `1200` to `600` or less. On mobile devices, disable `players` ESP and keep only `npcs` and `items` active to reduce the rendering load.

- ❌ **Mobile Executor Crashes on Inject**
  Free up at least 500 MB of RAM by closing background apps. Ensure the executor `.apk` or `.ipa` is the latest version and that overlay permissions are properly granted.
